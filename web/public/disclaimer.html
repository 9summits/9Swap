02Risk Disclaimer

Interacting with decentralised protocols carries serious risk, including the total and irreversible loss of the assets involved. Among the risks you accept:

  • Volatility. Token prices can move sharply between the moment a quote is displayed and the moment your transaction is mined.
  • Slippage. The amount you actually receive can be worse than the quoted amount, down to the minimum you accepted when setting your slippage tolerance.
  • MEV, sandwich attacks, and front-running. Public mempool transactions can be observed and reordered by third parties to your disadvantage.
  • Smart contract risk. Routers, settlement contracts, pools, bridges, and tokens are third-party code that may contain bugs, be exploited, be upgraded, or be malicious. We do not audit them.
  • Failed or stuck transactions. A transaction can revert and still cost gas, or remain pending indefinitely.
  • Malicious or counterfeit tokens. Any address can be imported or routed; a token symbol proves nothing. Verify contract addresses yourself.
  • Operational risk. Wrong chain, wrong address, compromised wallet, phishing site: none of it is recoverable. Always check the URL.

Fees

Using the interface itself is free, but a partner / referral fee may be included in the routes returned by some venues, via those venues' native referral mechanisms. Where applicable it is already reflected in the quoted output amount you see, so the comparison between venues stays like-for-like. On some venues (currently Velora), positive slippage above the quoted amount — the difference between the quote and a better execution — may be retained by the operator through that venue's native surplus mechanism; this does not reduce the quoted amount itself. You also pay network gas fees, and each venue may apply its own protocol or solver fees, which we do not control.

03Privacy

No accounts. No cookies. No trackers. No analytics script. No advertising. No personal data collection. We do not set a single cookie, we run no analytics or tracking script of any kind, and we build no profile of you. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log out of.

What passes through our infrastructure

To fetch quotes, your swap parameters — chain, token pair, amount, slippage, and your wallet address when one is connected — are sent to our serverless functions, which forward them to the third-party aggregator APIs and return the responses to your browser. That is the only purpose we use them for; we do not build a database or a per-user record from them.

Once you sign and broadcast a swap, your browser also reports the outcome to our /done endpoint. From that report we keep an aggregate, anonymous usage counter — the venue, the chain id, and the outcome (transaction sent, order placed, or error) — and nothing else: no wallet address, no amount, and no IP address is stored or aggregated, and no client-side analytics script runs to produce it. These counters live in our hosting and logging providers' server logs (currently Vercel, possibly forwarded to a log-management service) and are used only for aggregate usage statistics, never to identify or track an individual visitor.

Standard, transient infrastructure logs (request lines, error traces) may also be produced by our hosting provider for operational purposes.

Note that a wallet address is inherently public and pseudonymous on-chain data. The third-party aggregators, RPC providers, and price APIs that receive a request necessarily see it, and are governed by their own privacy policies.

What your browser talks to directly

  • Your wallet extension or app, and — only if you choose WalletConnect — its relay.
  • Public price and token-metadata APIs (for example CoinGecko) used to display reference rates and USD values.
  • Public token-icon CDNs (1inch, TrustWallet) used to display logos for tokens outside the curated list.
  • The blockchain RPC endpoint used to read balances and broadcast your signed transaction.

What stays on your device

Your browser's local storage holds a few convenience preferences — recently used tokens, tokens you imported manually, and your wallet connection state. This data stays in your browser, is never transmitted to us, and is removed when you clear your browser storage.
